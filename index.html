Why a hardware presence device?

PICO Human Presence Pro lives outside your OS. It appears as a standard USB keyboard + mouse, with behaviour tuned for real-world remote work rhythms.

Human-like activity

Keyboard sessions with typos & pauses

Text blocks are typed character by character with random delays, occasional typos and backspaces, and micro-pauses around punctuation — just like a real human writing feedback, notes or comments.

Configurable typo ratio
Mouse behaviour

Curved routes, clicks & idle windows

The mouse performs short sessions following smooth, curved paths with random clicks and pauses. Trails are slightly different every time, avoiding robotic straight-line movement.

How PICO Human Presence Pro works

The device is simple by design. No cloud, no external services — just a tiny microcontroller acting as a HID keyboard + mouse with a brain.

  • 1
    Flash the firmware once Install CircuitPython on your RP2040 board and copy the provided code.py file. From this moment, the board is a programmable HID device.
  • 2
    Configure texts & behaviour from desktop Run the Pico Human Presence Configurator. Detect the PICO drive, edit your 5–30 text blocks and tweak behaviour settings, then save. Everything is stored in a config.json on the device.
  • 3
    Plug into the “work” machine Connect the PICO to the machine that runs your monitoring, dashboards or time tracking software. It appears as a regular USB keyboard and mouse — no drivers required.
  • 4
    Toggle with the onboard button Press the board’s button once to start presence mode (NeoPixel turns green). The firmware cycles between keyboard sessions, mouse-only routes and idle windows according to your config. Press again to stop.

Designed for remote work reality

PICO Human Presence Pro is not about faking full-time work. It is for freelancers who genuinely deliver value but need protection against aggressive idle detection, flaky dashboards and “always-green” demands.

Important · read before using
Is this tool legal?

PICO Human Presence Pro is a generic HID device. In many jurisdictions it is legal to use hardware that sends keyboard and mouse input to your own computer. However, using it to breach contracts, falsify working hours or defraud clients or employers can be illegal and may have serious consequences. The responsibility is entirely yours.

Does it send data to the cloud?

No. The firmware runs on the microcontroller only. Configuration is kept in a local config.json file stored on the PICO drive. There is no telemetry, no remote control and no network connectivity inside the device.

What about company policies?

Many companies and platforms explicitly forbid any attempt to manipulate attendance or tracking tools. Before using PICO Human Presence Pro on a work machine, you should carefully review:

  • your employment or contractor agreement,
  • internal IT and security policies,
  • terms of service of any third-party platforms.

If in doubt, do not use it in that context.
